Project Case Study

Traces of the White Soul

Lead Game Tester / QA2D Platformer • Unity (older 5.x version, exact version unknown)
View GitHub Repository ↗

A university 2D platformer with three levels, enemies and boss encounters. My role on the project focused on quality assurance and systematic gameplay testing.

Testing

I was the main tester and tested every level, enemy and boss. My testing included unit testing and black-box testing, along with checking bugs, failure cases and gameplay behavior across the game.

My contribution

I focused on identifying issues and verifying the game from both component-level and player-facing perspectives. The project used an older Unity version, likely from the 5.x series; I do not recall the exact release.
