Project Case Study

SciLab VR

VR Game Designer / DeveloperUnity 6 • C# • Meta Quest 3 • English & Malay
View GitHub Repository ↗

SciLab VR is an immersive science-learning experience for Form 4 students, with guided experiments that let learners observe, measure and record results in a virtual environment.

Four playable levels

The first level takes place in a grassy field and explores how pulse changes by gender, age and activity. Students measure and enter readings before comparing them. The remaining experiments take place in a laboratory: comparing hollow and solid structures under load; tracking seedling growth over six days; investigating materials and latex coagulation; and observing how apple slices change over time.

My contribution

I designed the game logic and developed the gameplay, experiment interactions and recording mechanics for Levels 1–4. Activities include collecting measurements with controllers, entering and submitting results, and viewing calculated summaries such as the seedling mean.

Movement and platform

The experience supports English and Malay. Players move continuously with the left joystick and snap turn with the right; there is no player-controlled teleportation. Some activity transitions reposition the player in front of a new interface as required by the experience.
