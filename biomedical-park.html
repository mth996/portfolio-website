Project Case Study

Bio-Medical Park Malaysia

Solo Unity DeveloperUnity • 2D Walkthrough

A small interactive walkthrough created to present the layout of a biomedical park and give visitors a guided look at its research and development spaces.

Walkthrough

The 2D experience guides visitors through the park, including an R&D department and several rooms. Three videos are placed within the walkthrough to introduce the site and its work.

My contribution

I developed the complete application solo, laying out the rooms and the navigation flow and integrating the video content into the visitor experience.
