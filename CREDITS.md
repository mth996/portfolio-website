## Site owner's assets

The explorer characters (`models/explorer-*`) and the bikes (`models/bike-ride*`, `models/bike-park*`) were generated with Meshy AI and supplied by the site owner. For the web they were reduced to about 60,000 triangles, given WebP textures, and the bikes were aligned to a shared scale and orientation.
